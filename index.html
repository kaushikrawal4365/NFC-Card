FAQ

Questions businesses usually ask.

Keep this section short. Your sales conversation can handle anything more specific.

Do customers need an app?

No special AlwaysTap app is required. NFC-compatible phones can interact with an NFC tag/card. A QR backup can also be included.

Can I change the review destination?

Yes, depending on your setup. Use a redirect/landing-page approach if you want flexibility later.

Where should I place the card?

Reception, billing counter, checkout desk, table, waiting area or anywhere a customer naturally finishes their interaction.

Can I use it to get negative reviews too?

The safest positioning is to make the experience available to all customers and ask for genuine feedback. Do not offer rewards for positive reviews or suppress genuine negative feedback.
