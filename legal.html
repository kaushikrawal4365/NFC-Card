AlwaysTap is an enquiry-led business. This website does not collect payment credentials or process payments. Payment instructions, where applicable, are shared by our team through WhatsApp after an enquiry.

Privacy Policy

AlwaysTap collects information that you voluntarily submit through the enquiry form, such as your name and phone number. We use this information to respond to your enquiry, discuss the selected card package, arrange setup or delivery, and provide support.

We may record enquiry details, selected package, quantity and consent status in our business lead sheet. Access is limited to people who need it to handle enquiries. We do not ask for or intentionally store UPI PINs, OTPs, card numbers, banking passwords or other payment credentials.

We may retain enquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to manage the customer relationship, fulfil an order, provide support, maintain business records, or meet legal obligations. You can ask us to correct or delete your personal information, subject to information we must retain for legal or operational reasons.

To request access, correction or deletion, contact us on WhatsApp at +91 93429 05034. We may need to verify your identity before processing a request.

Terms and Conditions

By using this website or submitting an enquiry, you agree to provide accurate information and use the website lawfully. An enquiry is not an accepted order. An order is confirmed only after the details, price, availability, payment method and delivery terms are agreed with AlwaysTap through WhatsApp or another confirmed business channel.

AlwaysTap NFC cards are configured for the agreed destination or use case. Customers should verify the destination, merchant identity and amount before approving any payment or sharing information after tapping a card. NFC and QR experiences may require a compatible device, an internet connection and, where applicable, device unlock or payment-app approval.

Do not share your UPI PIN, OTP, card number, banking password or payment-app credentials with AlwaysTap or any other person.

Shipping and Delivery Policy

Delivery timelines, shipping charges and the delivery address will be confirmed through WhatsApp before an order is accepted. Delivery estimates may vary because of production, configuration, courier and address-related delays.

Customers should provide a complete and accurate delivery address and remain available to receive the shipment. If a shipment is returned because of an incorrect address or repeated missed delivery, additional shipping or re-dispatch charges may apply.

After receiving the card, please inspect it and contact us promptly if it is damaged, incorrect or unusable.

Cancellation and Refund Policy

Contact us on WhatsApp as soon as possible if you want to cancel an enquiry or confirmed order. Cancellation may not be possible after a card has been customised, configured, produced or dispatched.

For a damaged, defective or incorrectly supplied card, send photographs and order details through WhatsApp promptly after delivery. We will review the issue and, where appropriate, arrange a replacement or refund according to the agreed order terms and applicable law.

Refund timing depends on the original payment method and the processing timelines of the relevant bank or payment provider. Customised or used products may have different eligibility depending on the confirmed order terms.
